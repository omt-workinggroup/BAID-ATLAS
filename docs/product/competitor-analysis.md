# Propertiq Competitor Analysis

**Research date:** October 2026 (desk research carried out 18 October 2026)
**Scope:** Meqasa, Ghana Property Centre, Jiji Ghana (property), Zillow, Bayut, Property Finder

## Method and how to read this document

- Research used web search (standard and extended modes) over public pages: official sites, help centres, app-store listings, company blogs and press releases, plus third-party reviews (Trustpilot, ConsumerAffairs), trade press and vendor blogs.
- **Limitation:** the direct page fetcher could not resolve any of the six competitor domains from the research environment (DNS failure on meqasa.com, ghanapropertycentre.com, jiji.com.gh, bayut.com, propertyfinder.bh, zillow.com). Every finding therefore comes from search-engine summaries of the cited pages, not from reading the live page directly. Before anyone relies on a detail, re-check it on the live page, especially prices.
- **Labels:**
  - **CONFIRMED**: the cited source says it. The label records whether that source is the platform itself (official) or a third party. Being CONFIRMED means the source makes the statement. It does not mean the statement has been independently proven.
  - **ASSUMPTION**: our inference, or something we could not find a public source for.
- Self-reported performance figures from a platform (for example "5x more leads") are marked as marketing claims.
- Competitor blogs (for example Ownkey, a Ghana portal) are flagged where they are used, because they have a commercial interest.
- Prices are shown only where a source states them, with the currency. No official rate card was found for any platform except where noted.

## 1. Meqasa (Ghana)

**Main audience and categories**
- Property seekers in Ghana (renters, buyers, expats) and the agents and developers who list for them. CONFIRMED (official blog aimed at expats): https://blog.meqasa.com/3-things-expats-know-renting-buying-house-ghana/
- Categories cover houses and apartments for rent, sale or lease, and developer projects. CONFIRMED (homepage title "Houses for Sale, for Rent or to Lease in Ghana"): https://meqasa.com/. Land and commercial categories are an ASSUMPTION; we did not see them in the material reviewed.

**Search and discovery**
- Saved searches, property alerts and web-to-app account sync. CONFIRMED (App Store listing): https://apps.apple.com/gh/app/meqasa/id1401184947 and https://blog.meqasa.com/introducing-meqasa-mobile-apps/
- Location and other filters. CONFIRMED (official how-to): https://blog.meqasa.com/search-property-meqasa/
- Map-based search: not confirmed by any source found. ASSUMPTION: needs a check on the live site.
- Virtual tours: the blog lists developer projects that offer third-party virtual tours. We found no evidence of a native 3D or virtual tour tool. CONFIRMED (blog): https://blog.meqasa.com/properties-in-ghana-that-offer-a-virtual-tour/
- Property comparison tool: none found. ASSUMPTION.

**Verification and trust**
- Agents and developers must register with a valid Registrar-General business certificate and a valid national ID. CONFIRMED (official FAQ, via search summary): https://meqasa.com/faq
- A pay-to-list model is described as keeping "only serious agents and developers" on the platform. CONFIRMED (third-party profile, 2025): https://www.spotlightinafrica.com/post/meqasa-ghana-s-platform-modernising-property-market-through-technology-transparency
- The site shows which agents charge registration or viewing fees. CONFIRMED (official blog): https://blog.meqasa.com/why-some-agents-charge-registration-fees/
- An older official blog post admits Meqasa "can't vouch for everyone" and advises caution. A test-subdomain About page claims every property is reviewed. Because that page is on a test subdomain, it may not be current policy. CONFIRMED (sources as stated): https://blog.meqasa.com/3-renting-or-buying-houses-in-ghana-tips/amp/ and https://test.meqasa.com/about
- The site publishes scam-awareness content. CONFIRMED: https://blog.meqasa.com/how-to-identify-and-avoid-real-estate-scams/
- Listing-level verification badges, freshness indicators and a "report listing" flow: none found. ASSUMPTION: needs a check on the live site.

**Listing and marketing tools**
- The Meqasa Agent app is a portfolio dashboard with a leads search tool and "full control over promotion features". CONFIRMED (App Store): https://apps.apple.com/us/app/meqasa-agent/id1440981475
- The listing flow includes currency choice, commission type, amenities and photo upload, with at least 3 photos recommended and 10+ ideal. CONFIRMED (official blog; post is several years old): https://blog.meqasa.com/list-property-10-steps/

**Monetisation**
- Agents and developers pay to list. After sign-up, a sales executive gets in touch to discuss plan options and charges. CONFIRMED (official FAQ): https://meqasa.com/faq
- No public prices were found. A two-week trial is mentioned in one search summary, but we could not tie it to a primary source, so it remains an ASSUMPTION.

**Strengths**
- A Ghana-specific brand with long tenure, and identity and business-registration checks on advertisers. CONFIRMED (FAQ above).
- Separate apps for seekers and agents, with alerts. CONFIRMED (App Store).

**Weaknesses and user problems to investigate**
- Pricing is opaque and sales-led, which may put off small or independent agents. ASSUMPTION based on the absence of public prices.
- Advertisers are verified, but individual listings do not appear to be. The platform's own blog concedes limits. CONFIRMED (blog above); how much this matters in practice is an ASSUMPTION.
- No public body of user reviews or complaints was found. ASSUMPTION: run user interviews.

**Opportunity for Propertiq**
- Add listing-level checks (document review, on-site photo or availability check, expiry dates) on top of advertiser-level checks, and publish transparent self-serve pricing.

## 2. Ghana Property Centre (ghanapropertycentre.com)

**Main audience and categories**
- Buyers and renters in Ghana. Listings come from estate agents and developers "you can contact directly". CONFIRMED (official listing pages): https://ghanapropertycentre.com/all-property
- Categories are flats, houses, land and commercial property for sale and rent. The site showed about 9,492 listings in total, with about 7,354 for sale and 3,594 for rent, at the time of indexing (around September 2026). CONFIRMED: https://ghanapropertycentre.com/all-property, https://ghanapropertycentre.com/for-sale, https://ghanapropertycentre.com/for-rent
- Part of the same network as Nigeria Property Centre, which says it operates in Ghana, Kenya, Uganda and Ethiopia. CONFIRMED (official NPC page): https://nigeriapropertycentre.com/about-us

**Search and discovery**
- Category, region and property-type pages, plus keyword refinement (for example "gated communities" in Accra). CONFIRMED: https://ghanapropertycentre.com/for-sale/houses/greater-accra/showtype?n=gated+communities
- Saved-search email alerts ("We'll email you when a new listing matches..."), saved listings, and messaging with agents. CONFIRMED (search and sign-in pages): https://ghanapropertycentre.com/for-sale and https://ghanapropertycentre.com/login
- Map search, virtual tours and comparison: none found. ASSUMPTION.

**Verification and trust**
- "Verified Agent" badges appear on the Nigerian sister site. We did not see them on the Ghana pages reviewed. CONFIRMED (observation in search results); whether Ghana has them is an ASSUMPTION.
- Scam warnings and listing-report tools: none found in the indexed pages. ASSUMPTION: needs a check on the live site.

**Listing and marketing tools**
- Paid promotion tags are visible, including "Premium" and "Premium Plus Top promoted listing". CONFIRMED (listing pages above).
- Agent dashboard and lead tools: not documented in the sources found. ASSUMPTION: likely similar to NPC's agent accounts.

**Monetisation**
- Paid promoted placements. CONFIRMED (tags above). Featured listings are a paid product across the network, and agents may pay for premium listings. CONFIRMED (NPC services page): https://nigeriapropertycentre.com/our-services
- No published Ghana prices were found.

**Strengths**
- Large listing count, a simple category structure, alerts, and the backing of a pan-African network. CONFIRMED (above).

**Weaknesses and user problems to investigate**
- Trust signals on the Ghana pages are not visible. ASSUMPTION.
- A competitor blog (Ownkey) claims that portals of this kind carry duplicates and inactive listings. The claim comes from a competitor with a commercial interest and remains unverified for GPC: https://ownkey.com/blog/best-real-estate-portals-africa
- Listing freshness, and whether listing counts reflect units that are actually available. ASSUMPTION: worth sampling.

**Opportunity for Propertiq**
- Show visible freshness ("confirmed available on [date]") and de-duplication, and explain each badge in plain language.

## 3. Jiji Ghana (property section)

**Main audience and categories**
- A general classifieds marketplace for individuals and businesses. Its property category covers houses and apartments for rent (including self-contain, shared apartments and studios), and also items for sale. CONFIRMED (category pages): https://jiji.com.gh/42-self-contain-for-rent, https://jiji.com.gh/42-shared-apartment-for-rent, https://jiji.com.gh/houses-apartments-for-rent/furnished
- Land and commercial categories are an ASSUMPTION, typical for Jiji but not checked.

**Search and discovery**
- Facet pages by bedroom count, furnished status and area (for example "1 bedroom", "furnished", Ghanata Area). CONFIRMED: https://jiji.com.gh/houses-apartments-for-rent/1-bedroom
- Map search, saved-search alerts and virtual tours for property: none found. ASSUMPTION.

**Verification and trust**
- Listings show a "Verified ID" tag and seller tenure (for example "5+ years on Jiji"). Some listing pages group "Verified sellers". CONFIRMED (listing pages above). What "Verified ID" actually checks is not documented in the sources found. ASSUMPTION: it most likely checks seller identity, not the property or the right to let it.
- Scam reports go by email to support@jiji.com.gh, with a link to the user's profile. CONFIRMED (via search summary of Jiji FAQ): https://jiji.com.gh/faq/32
- Seller tier labels ("DIAMOND", "ENTERPRISE") appear on listings. These are paid tiers, not trust signals, and users could confuse the two. CONFIRMED (labels); the confusion risk is an ASSUMPTION.

**Listing and marketing tools**
- Two kinds of premium promotion: TOP placement and Boost packages. The Boost tiers are Basic, Premium, VIP, VIP Gold, and Diamond & Enterprise. Jiji claims Diamond & Enterprise brings "up to 500 times more clients" (marketing claim). CONFIRMED (Jiji Ghana FAQ): https://jiji.com.gh/faq/91
- Boost durations of 1, 3, 6 or 12 months. CONFIRMED only for Jiji Nigeria: https://jiji.ng/faq/22. That the same applies in Ghana is an ASSUMPTION.

**Monetisation**
- Free basic listing, with paid TOP and Boost packages. CONFIRMED (FAQ above). Prices depend on category, duration and option. CONFIRMED (third-party): https://productmint.com/how-does-jiji-make-money/
- No published GH₵ prices were found.

**Strengths**
- Very large general audience, a low barrier to listing, and strong coverage of budget rentals such as self-contain and shared units. CONFIRMED that the categories exist; the audience size claim is an ASSUMPTION.

**Weaknesses and user problems to investigate**
- Trustpilot and Play Store reviewers complain about pressure to buy premium ("force you to pay for premium or your product won't show") and about calls they believe are from staff posing as buyers. CONFIRMED (user reviews; anecdotal): https://www.trustpilot.com/review/jiji.com.gh, https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.olx.ssa.gh&hl=en_US
- Fake-agent rental scams that move victims to WhatsApp have been reported on the Nigerian sister site. CONFIRMED (anecdotal, Nigeria): https://www.trustpilot.com/review/jiji.ng. That the same pattern occurs in Ghana is an ASSUMPTION.
- Ghana property fraud patterns include unlicensed agents, payment to personal MoMo numbers, and "reserve now" deposits. CONFIRMED (competitor blog Ownkey, which has a commercial interest): https://ownkey.com/gh/blog/how-to-avoid-property-fraud-ghana

**Opportunity for Propertiq**
- Serve the budget rental segment with in-app reporting, REAC licence display, and warnings about off-platform payment, while keeping paid promotion visually separate from trust badges.

## 4. Zillow (USA)

**Main audience and categories**
- US home buyers, sellers, renters and agents. Categories are for-sale homes, rentals and new construction. CONFIRMED (app listing): https://apps.apple.com/us/app/zillow-real-estate-rentals/id310738695

**Search and discovery**
- Advanced filters, saved searches with instant or daily email alerts, Zestimate change alerts, and alerts for listings with 3D Home tours. CONFIRMED (official guide): https://www.zillow.com/learn/zillow-advanced-search/
- 3D Home tours, plus SkyTour (an interactive 3D exterior built from drone footage) on Showcase listings captured through the Media Experts premium package (July 2025). CONFIRMED (official): https://www.zillow.com/news/zillow-showcase-brings-listings-to-life/ and https://zillow.mediaroom.com/2025-07-15-Summer-just-got-hotter-Zillow-debuts-five-powerful-new-features
- Offer Insights (July 2025) compares how different offer prices might perform, using the Zestimate and local data. CONFIRMED (same press release).
- Map-centric search. CONFIRMED (the Android app package is "zillowmap"): https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.zillow.android.zillowmap&hl=en_US

**Verification and trust**
- A "Report Listing" option on listing pages, including a fraud option, plus a contact form for suspicious rentals. CONFIRMED (Zillow help centre and local news quoting Zillow): https://www.zillow.help/categories/Renter-Landlord-Property-Manager/Landlord-Property-Manager/Listing-Rental-Management and https://www.koaa.com/money/consumer/consumers-should-double-check-rental-listings-for-red-flags-and-signs-of-fraud
- A third-party site says Zillow's terms do not guarantee listing accuracy. CONFIRMED (third-party, not checked against the terms): https://flagmylisting.com/blog/zillow-rental-scams
- For-sale listings mostly come from MLS feeds, which helps keep them fresh. ASSUMPTION (industry knowledge, not sourced here).

**Listing and marketing tools**
- Premier Agent (buyer leads for agents), Flex (pay a referral fee at closing), Showcase premium listings (Zillow claims 79% more page views; marketing figure), AI virtual staging (September 2025), and Zillow Rental Manager for landlords. CONFIRMED (Showcase and staging, official): https://www.zillow.com/news/zillow-showcase-brings-listings-to-life/. Flex model, CONFIRMED (third-party): https://convin.ai/blog/how-does-zillow-flex-work-agent-needs

**Monetisation**
- Premier Agent: market-based lead pricing with no public list price. Flex: third-party sources put the referral fee anywhere from 15% to 40% of commission, so the figures conflict. CONFIRMED (third-party): https://listwithclever.com/real-estate-blog/how-much-are-zillow-leads/, https://convin.ai/blog/how-does-zillow-flex-work-agent-needs
- Rental Manager: third-party reviews give conflicting figures, such as USD 9.99 per property per week for extra listings, and Premium at USD 29.99 for 90 days or from USD 39.99. CONFIRMED (third-party, conflicting): https://www.turbotenant.com/reviews/zillow-rental-manager-review/, https://www.landlordstudio.com/blog/zillow-rental-manager-review. Treat these as unverified.

**Strengths**
- Deep data (Zestimate, sale history), mature alerts, and leading immersive media (3D, SkyTour, virtual staging). CONFIRMED (above).

**Weaknesses and user problems to investigate**
- A September 2025 class action alleges that "Contact Agent" routes buyers to Zillow-affiliated agents rather than the listing agent, and that Flex fees run up to 40%. These are allegations, not findings. CONFIRMED (reported): https://www.inman.com/2025/11/10/zillow-hit-with-new-class-action-suit-over-alleged-broker-kickbacks/
- Consumer complaints about inaccurate listings and slow status corrections. CONFIRMED (ConsumerAffairs reviews, anecdotal): https://www.consumeraffairs.com/homeowners/zillowcom.html?page=6
- Agents report declining lead quality and shared leads. CONFIRMED (agent blog, anecdotal): https://jefflenney.com/real-estate/is-zillow-premier-agent-worth-it/

**Opportunity for Propertiq**
- Be explicit about who the user is contacting (the listing agent, an owner or a partner), and set a clear process and target time for correcting listing errors. Treat Zillow as a reference for media and alerts, not as a Ghana competitor.

## 5. Bayut (UAE)

**Main audience and categories**
- UAE property buyers, renters and investors, plus agents and developers. Bayut also runs a Saudi site. Categories are residential and commercial, for rent and sale, plus off-plan. CONFIRMED (official): https://www.bayut.com/mybayut/about-bayut-uae/ and https://www.bayut.sa/blog/en/invest-in-saudi/trucheck/. The off-plan and commercial detail is an ASSUMPTION.

**Search and discovery**
- Map view with schools, metro and workplace context, and commute-time search. CONFIRMED (official): https://www.bayut.com/mybayut/bayut-app-great-app-uae-property-search/
- 3D virtual tours and floor plans. BayutGPT, an AI search assistant. CONFIRMED (App Store): https://apps.apple.com/us/app/bayut-uae-property-search/id923263211
- TruEstimate valuation using Dubai Land Department (DLD) data, and a Dubai Transactions explorer with email alerts. CONFIRMED (official and press): https://www.bayut.com/mybayut/truestimate-property-valuation-bayut/, https://www.zawya.com/en/press-release/companies-news/bayut-launches-property-valuation-tool-truestimate-with-data-integration-from-dubai-land-department-kyhij0v8, https://www.bayut.com/agentportal/use-bayuts-tools-and-get-ahead-as-an-area-specialist/
- Saved property searches: not explicitly confirmed. ASSUMPTION.

**Verification and trust**
- **Checked** badge: the agent uploads documents (title deed, owner passport or Emirates ID, Form A or NOC), and Bayut's QA team verifies them. CONFIRMED (official): https://www.bayut.com/mybayut/trucheck-bayut-uae
- **TruCheck** badge: the agent photographs the unit on site using the BayutPro or Profolio app, and Bayut QC confirms availability. The badge is valid for 30 days, and TruCheck listings are ranked highest within their tier. Bayut claims 5x leads and 20x impressions (marketing claim). CONFIRMED (official, same page).
- TruCheck filter and a "Report this Property" button. CONFIRMED (Bayut responses on the App Store): https://apps.apple.com/us/app/bayut-uae-property-search/id923263211?see-all=reviews&platform=iphone

**Listing and marketing tools**
- Profolio app for agents. SmartLeads. Listing tiers Signature, Hot and Standard. Area-specialist tools. CONFIRMED (official): https://www.bayut.com/mybayut/new-features-checked-trucheck-smartleads-bayutpro/, https://www.bayut.com/agentportal/bayut/

**Monetisation**
- Agency subscriptions with credits and tiered listing upgrades. CONFIRMED that the tiers exist (Signature, Hot, Standard); the subscription-plus-credit model is CONFIRMED only by third parties.
- No official rate card was found. A vendor blog estimates AED 2,500–12,000+ per month per office. This is an unverified planning estimate: https://www.groovyweb.co/blog/bayut-vs-property-finder-leads

**Strengths**
- The most explicit verification model of the platforms reviewed, with defined documents, an on-site check and a 30-day expiry. Strong use of government data. CONFIRMED (above).

**Weaknesses and user problems to investigate**
- Trustpilot reviewers describe "ghost" listings (already rented or non-existent), reported listings that stayed up, and a claim that agencies are not verified. CONFIRMED (user reviews, anecdotal): https://uk.trustpilot.com/review/bayut.com
- Analysts argue that bait listings persist because agents pay portals for leads. CONFIRMED (opinion piece): https://dubaiprimex.com/articles/bayut-vs-property-finder-vs-dubizzle/
- Verified and unverified listings sit side by side, so a strong badge does not by default clean up the whole feed. ASSUMPTION.

**Opportunity for Propertiq**
- Adapt the two-step Checked/TruCheck idea (documents, then a dated availability check with expiry) to Ghana's realities: Lands Commission documents and indentures, and REAC licences. Consider making some verified level the default rather than optional.

## 6. Property Finder (UAE and MENA)

**Main audience and categories**
- Buyers, renters and investors in the UAE, Bahrain, Egypt, Saudi Arabia and Qatar, plus agents and developers. Residential and commercial, rent and sale, off-plan. CONFIRMED (regional support sites): https://support.propertyfinder.bh/hc/en-us/articles/25260902111762, https://support.propertyfinder.eg/hc/en-us/articles/16678819876114-PF-Flex-credit-per-listing-update. The Saudi and Qatar markets are an ASSUMPTION.

**Search and discovery**
- Filters (bedrooms, bathrooms, price, location, amenities), maps with price filters, and direct contact by phone, SMS, WhatsApp or email. 2025 releases added a monthly-rent option, a home valuation tool and "smarter search". CONFIRMED (app stores): https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=ae.propertyfinder.propertyfinder&hl=en_US, https://apps.apple.com/us/app/property-finder-real-estate/id897540233
- A "Verified" filter and a SuperAgent filter. CONFIRMED (developer replies on the App Store; launch coverage): https://apps.apple.com/us/app/property-finder-real-estate/id897540233?see-all=reviews&platform=iphone, https://onlinemarketplaces.com/articles/property-finder-launches-agent-ranking-system-to-benefit-users-in-the-uae

**Verification and trust**
- **Verified listings**: verification aims to ensure the photos match the actual property. CONFIRMED (app listing). The exact checks are not publicly specified in the sources found.
- **SuperAgent** is an agent-level badge, recalculated monthly. In Bahrain the criteria include a verified agent profile, 5+ active listings, 10+ leads in 60 days, a listing quality score of at least 90%, a 2-hour WhatsApp response target and an 80% response rate. CONFIRMED (official Bahrain help centre): https://support.propertyfinder.bh/hc/en-us/articles/25260902111762
- Validates Dubai Trakheesi advertising permit numbers on listings. CONFIRMED (official blog): https://www.propertyfinder.ae/blog/trakheesi/
- "Report" button for misleading listings. CONFIRMED (App Store developer replies).

**Listing and marketing tools**
- PF Expert (agent platform) with a cost calculator. Standard, featured and premium listings for 15 or 30 days. PF Flex credits. CONFIRMED (official Egypt help centre; article about 2 years old): https://support.propertyfinder.eg/hc/en-us/articles/16678819876114-PF-Flex-credit-per-listing-update, https://www.propertyfinder.com/terms-conditions-pf-flex/
- CRM integrations exist through third parties. CONFIRMED (third-party): https://www.retyn.ai/blog/best-propertyfinder-real-estate-crm-streamline-property-listings

**Monetisation**
- Credits per listing, with the cost varying by community according to leads per listing, competition and seasonality. CONFIRMED (official Egypt help centre).
- No official AED rate card was found. Vendor estimates put spend at AED 3,000–15,000+ per month per office, and AED 500–2,000+ per listing. These are unverified: https://www.groovyweb.co/blog/bayut-vs-property-finder-leads

**Strengths**
- Transparent, measurable agent-quality criteria, including responsiveness. Integration with a government permit system. CONFIRMED (above).

**Weaknesses and user problems to investigate**
- App reviews complain about fake listings and about not being able to select several property types at once. CONFIRMED (user reviews, anecdotal): https://apps.apple.com/us/app/property-finder-real-estate/id897540233?see-all=reviews&platform=iphone
- Credit costs change dynamically, which makes budgeting hard for agencies. ASSUMPTION drawn from the variable-credit model.

**Opportunity for Propertiq**
- Publish agent-quality criteria in the same measurable way, including response time to leads. Ghana has no Trakheesi-style permit, so Propertiq would need its own document and licence checks (REAC register, Lands Commission search status). ASSUMPTION: what can be checked depends on what data REAC and the Lands Commission make accessible.

## Where Propertiq can differentiate (realistically)

These are directions to test with users and agents. They are not claims that Propertiq is or will be better than these platforms.

1. **Listing-level verification with expiry, adapted to Ghana.** Bayut's documents-then-on-site model (Checked, then TruCheck valid for 30 days) is the clearest public example. None of the Ghana portals reviewed publicly documents a listing-level check. A Ghana version could combine indenture or title documents, an owner or agent mandate, and a dated availability visit. It needs to be piloted for cost and agent acceptance.
2. **Badges that say exactly what was checked, and when.** Each badge would carry a plain-language explanation and a date, instead of an unexplained "Verified" label (Jiji's "Verified ID" is undocumented in public sources).
3. **Keeping paid promotion visually separate from trust.** Jiji's "DIAMOND" and GPC's "Premium Plus" tags could be read as quality signals. Propertiq could keep promotion and verification visually and semantically distinct.
4. **Visible REAC licence status for agents.** REAC keeps a public register under Act 1047. Linking or displaying licence status is feasible, depending on REAC data access (ASSUMPTION).
5. **Freshness and de-duplication by default.** A "last confirmed available" date, automatic expiry of unconfirmed listings, and duplicate detection, answering the ghost-listing complaints seen on Bayut and Zillow and the duplicate claims made about African portals.
6. **Transparent, self-serve pricing in GH₵.** Meqasa is sales-led, and GPC and Jiji publish no prices. Published plans could lower the barrier for independent agents and small landlords. The actual price points still need validating.
7. **Measurable agent-quality signals, including response time.** Modelled on Property Finder's SuperAgent criteria (response rate and listing quality score), adapted to WhatsApp-first communication in Ghana.
8. **Safety built into the contact flow.** Warnings against paying deposits to personal MoMo numbers, in-app reporting with acknowledged follow-up, and a clear statement of who the user is contacting (in contrast to the routing concerns raised in the Zillow lawsuit allegations).
9. **Serving the diaspora and remote buyers realistically.** Dated photo and video walkthroughs and document-status summaries, rather than promising full 3D tours at launch. Meqasa currently surfaces only third-party developer tours.
