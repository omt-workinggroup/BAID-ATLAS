Trust and verification

What Propertiq checks, and what it doesn't.

Every listing shows five separate lines instead of one "verified" badge. Each line says what was recorded, when, and what it does not prove.

1. Advertiser identity

What it means: Propertiq checked who the advertiser is, within the scope written next to the label (for example, "business registration matches the organisation name"). The date of the check and its expiry are shown.

What it doesn't prove: that the advertiser owns the property, or has the right to sell or rent it.

2. Property visit

What it means: a Propertiq representative or approved partner visited the property. When recorded, the listing shows the date, the type of visit and what was looked at.

What it doesn't prove: the building's structural condition, its value, or legal title. A visit is not a survey or a valuation.

Property visits are not recorded yet. Until they are, every listing honestly shows "Not recorded".

3. Documents

What it means: specific documents were looked at, within a stated scope. The listing names the document types and the review date.

What it doesn't prove: legal title. A document review is not a title certification. Confirm title with the Lands Commission or a qualified lawyer before paying.

Document reviews are not recorded yet. Until they are, every listing shows "Not reviewed".

4. Photos

What it means: where photos came from. Today all photos are uploaded by the advertiser, and renders or virtually staged images must be labelled as such.

What it doesn't prove: that the photos show the property's current condition.

5. Availability

What it means: the date the property was last confirmed as still on offer, and who confirmed it. Advertisers confirm when they submit a listing and can reconfirm at any time.

What it doesn't prove: that it is still available today. After 30 days without a confirmation, the listing says it needs reconfirming.

Payment never buys a check.

Advertisers can pay for sponsored placement, which is always labelled "Sponsored". It never changes, skips or speeds up any check above, and sponsored listings follow the same review rules as every other listing.

Before you pay anything

  • View the property in person, or through someone you trust.
  • Ask for identification and proof that the person may sell or rent the property.
  • Verify title documents at the Lands Commission or with a lawyer.
  • Never pay a deposit under pressure or before a viewing. Propertiq never asks you to pay outside the platform.
