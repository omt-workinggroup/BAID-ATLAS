How a listing gets on Propertiq

Three checks happen before you see anything. Paying for promotion doesn't skip any of them.

  1. 01

    The provider is checked

    Before an agency, developer or landlord can publish, we check who they are. We publish exactly what we checked and when the check expires.

  2. 02

    The listing is reviewed

    A moderator reads every listing before it goes live: photos, price, size and location. Renders and staged photos must be labelled.

  3. 03

    It goes live, clearly labelled

    Prices always show their currency, areas show their units, and the exact address stays private until you're in touch with the provider.

Verified means something specific here.

Each verification label says what was checked and what it doesn't prove. Labels expire, and paid promotion is always marked sponsored. It never counts as verification.
