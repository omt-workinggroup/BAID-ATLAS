Privacy Notice

Propertiq is run by Resilience Estate Ltd, Airport West, Accra. This notice explains what we collect when you create an account to list property, and why.

What we collect

  • Your name, email address, phone number and account type.
  • The details for your account type: for example your licence numbers, agency, company registration, or the identity document you choose.
  • The documents you upload, such as a licence, an identity document or an owner's authorisation.

Why

To create your account, to check that you are who you say you are and are allowed to list the property you add, and to contact you about your application.

Who can see it

Your documents and identity numbers are kept in private storage. Only you and the Propertiq team members who review applications can open them. They are never shown on your public profile. Your name, account type and the profile details you choose to show (like a company name, logo or website) appear publicly once you're approved.

Where it's kept

Accounts and documents are stored with our hosting provider, Supabase. Text messages with your sign-up code are sent through our SMS provider.

Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct or delete your information at any time: email info@resilienceghana.com or call +233 54 953 9092.
